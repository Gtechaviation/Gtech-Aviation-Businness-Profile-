Introduction

Welcome to Gtech Group Limited

GTECH Group Limited is a leading Bangladeshi multidisciplinary company with 20+ years of industry experience and a workforce of 240+ professionals, delivering innovative solutions across engineering, infrastructure, energy, healthcare, aviation, and hospitality sectors. With an annual turnover exceeding BDT 450 Crore, GTECH has established itself as a trusted partner for government, private, and multinational organizations.

The company has successfully executed projects in solar energy, civil construction, dredging, roads, highways, railways, bridges, medical equipment supply, and large-scale infrastructure development. Under the leadership of Chairman Mr. Mostafa Al Mahmud — an accomplished leader with a Master's degree from the University of Dhaka — GTECH continues to diversify its portfolio both locally and internationally, committed to quality, innovation, sustainability, and operational excellence.

"Engineering Growth. Connecting Industries. Building a Sustainable Future."
