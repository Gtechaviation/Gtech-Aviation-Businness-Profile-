Engineering, Energy & Infrastructure Solutions

Gtech Solution Limited · A Concern of GTECH Group Limited

Gtech Solution Limited is the flagship and engineering powerhouse of GTECH Group — a leading multidisciplinary company established in 2016, specializing in solar power engineering, EPC contracts, civil construction, dredging, roads, highways, railways & bridges, and medical equipment supply. We deliver complete, end-to-end solar solutions — project consultancy & feasibility studies, engineering design, EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction), performance analytics, remote monitoring and O&M.

With 7,000+ kWp of solar installed, GTECH has executed landmark EPC and IPP projects — including the 50 MW RPCL project (Madarganj, Jamalpur), the 35 MWp Spectra Solar Power Plant (Manikganj) and the 50 MWp Mymensingh Solar Power Plant — alongside iconic rooftop systems at Aptech Industrial Park (1,500 kWp), Karim Spinning Mills (1,287 kWp) and Habitus Fashion (1,147 kWp). Together these have saved 2 GWh of energy, mitigated 15,670+ tonnes of CO₂ and benefited 12,540+ people. The vertical unites Gtech Solar, Gtech LED, Allied Solar Energy, Vincen Gtech, DUPNO International & MNS Technology.

Infrastructure, Construction & Development

Gtech Infrastructure Ltd · A Concern of GTECH Group Limited

GPS Tracking & Digital Services

DUPNO International Limited · A Concern of GTECH Group Limited

The group's digital services and technology venture — GPS vehicle tracking, fleet management, software, and connected digital solutions that help businesses cut costs, improve efficiency, and stay in control in real time.

From private cars and motorcycles to full corporate fleets, DUPNO's BTRC-approved tracking platform delivers live location, engine on/off control, geo-fencing, route history, and fuel & driver-behaviour insights — all through easy-to-use mobile and web apps. Businesses rely on DUPNO to protect their vehicles, reduce operating costs, and make smarter decisions with real-time data, backed by nationwide support and affordable service packages for every budget.

Beyond vehicle tracking, DUPNO offers a complete telematics product line — OBD plug-and-play trackers, dash cameras with live video tracking, temperature monitoring for cold-chain transport, and field-force ID tracking for workforce management. As a BTRC-approved service provider and a member of the Vehicle Tracking Service Providers Association of Bangladesh (VTSPAB), DUPNO serves individuals and leading corporate houses alike with reliable, secure, and scalable tracking technology.

Aviation Opportunity & Strategy

The market opportunity in Bangladesh and beyond — why we entered aviation, why we are capable, and the vision and mission guiding us.

🛫

Aviation Market: Bangladesh

One of the fastest-growing aviation markets in South Asia, driven by strong economic expansion and global trade integration.

  • Rapid growth in passenger travel (domestic + international)
  • Strong export-driven air cargo demand (garments, pharma, agro)
  • Strategic location between Asia, Middle East & Europe
  • Ongoing airport modernization and infrastructure expansion
  • Rising demand for professional aviation support services
Bangladesh is evolving into a regional aviation and logistics hub.
🌏

Global High-Growth Aviation Markets

Globally, aviation is one of the fastest-recovering and expanding industries.

  • Middle East — Dubai, Qatar & Saudi hubs leading global connectivity
  • Southeast Asia — Singapore, Malaysia & Vietnam as logistics centers
  • South Asia — India & Bangladesh showing strong expansion
  • Africa — Rapid growth in infrastructure and new airlines
  • Europe & USA — Aviation technology, automation & efficiency
Global aviation is shifting toward integrated, technology-driven ecosystems.
🌱

Community Impact & Value Creation

Aviation development creates strong social and economic impact beyond business growth.

  • Job creation in aviation, logistics & technical sectors
  • Skill development and aviation training opportunities
  • Improved connectivity between cities and regions
  • Stronger export efficiency for industries and farmers
  • Emergency medical and cargo support systems
  • Technology-driven safety and operational efficiency
Aviation is a national development engine, not just a business sector.

Why We Are Interested in the Aviation Sector

We are highly interested in the aviation sector because it is one of the most critical drivers of modern economic growth, global connectivity, and international trade. Aviation is not just a transport industry — it is a strategic ecosystem that connects people, businesses, and nations.

Bangladesh is currently experiencing rapid growth in air travel, cargo movement, and infrastructure development, but the sector still lacks fully integrated, modern, and technology-driven service providers. This creates a strong opportunity for us to contribute with our experience in engineering, infrastructure development, logistics, and large-scale project execution.

Our interest is based on:

  • Strong market growth and long-term demand potential
  • National importance in trade, logistics, and connectivity
  • Gap in integrated aviation service providers in Bangladesh
  • Opportunity to apply our existing expertise in infrastructure and operations
  • Alignment with global aviation transformation trends
